# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People who want a straightforward way to capture tasks and keep track of deadlines before work slips through the cracks.

## Product Purpose
Help users add, review, and complete tasks quickly, with clear deadline context and low friction. Success looks like users being able to understand what matters next at a glance and act on it immediately.

## Brand Personality
Practical, focused, dependable. The interface should feel calm and useful, with enough urgency to support deadline-driven work without becoming stressful.

## Anti-references
Avoid overly minimal task UI that hides urgency, state, or actions. Avoid playful or gamified styling that makes the app feel less reliable for everyday productivity.

## Design Principles
1. Make urgency easy to scan without overwhelming the user.
2. Keep primary actions close to the task they affect.
3. Favor clarity and momentum over decorative complexity.
4. Preserve calm visual rhythm while still distinguishing important states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA basics for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard interaction, and touch-friendly controls. Motion should stay subtle and should not be required to understand task state.
